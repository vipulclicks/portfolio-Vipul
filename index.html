Case Study

pHinder

pH Made Simple — a chemistry education app that makes pH concepts intuitive.

The Problem

Understanding pH and acid-base chemistry is a challenge for many students. Existing educational tools are either overly simplistic or buried in textbook-style interfaces that don't engage learners. pHinder reimagines chemistry education with an interactive, visually rich iOS experience.

Key Features

  • Interactive pH scale with real-time calculation feedback
  • Guided onboarding using TipKit for progressive feature discovery
  • Visual acid-base indicator simulations
  • Clean, distraction-free interface designed for focused learning

Case Study

AstraFi

A personal finance app for early-to-mid career Indian professionals.

The Problem

Most finance apps are either too complex for beginners or too generic to be useful. AstraFi bridges the gap — giving young Indian professionals clear visibility into their net worth trajectory and financial health, with actionable projections instead of raw numbers.

Key Features

  • Net worth projection with customizable growth scenarios
  • Financial health score based on savings, debt, and investment ratios
  • Clean dashboard with at-a-glance financial overview
